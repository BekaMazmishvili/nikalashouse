Our Story

A Small House with a Warm Welcome

Nikala's House was created for travelers who want more than just a place to sleep. We wanted a place where guests feel welcomed, rested, and connected to the calm of Borjomi.

Hospitality That Feels Like Family

From the moment you arrive, we want you to feel at home. We take care of the small details — a clean room, fresh towels, a warm common area, and honest local advice — so you can focus on enjoying Borjomi.

Our guests come back for the quiet mornings, the friendly conversations, and the feeling that this house was made for people who appreciate simple, genuine comfort.

Close to Nature and the Town

Borjomi is famous for its mineral water, its beautiful central park, and its surrounding forests and mountains. Nikala's House sits in a quiet spot that keeps you close to the town while letting you escape into nature whenever you want.

Enjoy morning coffee on the balcony with a view over the mountains, or relax in our garden gazebo with a wood-fired oven — the perfect place for a slow evening after a day of exploring.
